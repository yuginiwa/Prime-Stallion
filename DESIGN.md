## Overview

Building Strength. Creating Legacy. The approved metallic artwork stays intact on a quiet dark surface. SEE HOW WE BUILD introduces project evidence, not the corporate tagline.

## Colors

Black and charcoal anchor the profile. Prime Gold signals the principal action. The lighter portfolio section gives real photographs and factual scopes room to read.

## Typography

Arial Bold and Regular are the approved working fallback. Use uppercase microcopy, sentence-case headings and no decorative shadow.

## Layout

Constrain layouts to the shared content width. On narrow screens, place copy and action before the hero image. Keep CTA labels readable without hover.

## Components

Use a filled gold action for inquiries, an outlined action for secondary navigation, and plain underlined text for inline actions. Project cards state locality and service lane.
